# Voice & tone (adapt the attributes to your brand)

## Voice — constant
Three to five attributes that NEVER change across channels. Examples:
- **Direct**: say what we mean in the first sentence
- **Warm**: address the reader, not a category
- **Plain**: jargon only when it's the precise word
- **Honest**: name trade-offs; don't oversell

## Tone — flexes by context
| Context | Tone modifier |
|---|---|
| Marketing landing | Confident, energized |
| Product UI copy | Calm, instructional |
| Error message | Patient, specific, no blame |
| Customer support | Empathetic, ownership ("we" not "you") |
| Outage post-mortem | Frank, accountable |

## Do / Don't (examples — replace with yours)
| Don't | Do |
|---|---|
| "Synergize your workflow" | "Connect your tools and stop copy-pasting" |
| "Unfortunately, an error occurred" | "We couldn't save that. Try again — your text is preserved." |
| "World-class platform" | "Used by 12,000 teams" |
| "🔥🚀 Game-changer 🔥🚀" | (just don't) |

## The sniff test (apply to any sentence)
- Does this sentence pass the **read-it-aloud test** — does it sound like a person?
- If a competitor put their logo on this copy, would it still make sense for them? If yes, it's too generic for you.
