# From notes to insights — affinity mapping

## Step 1: Atomize
Convert every observation into one sticky note (Miro / FigJam):
- "U3 looked for 'Profile' under 'Account'"
- "U5: 'I expected a save button at the top'"
- One observation per note. No interpretation.

## Step 2: Cluster
Group notes that describe the same behavior or feeling. Let clusters emerge bottom-up; don't pre-decide categories.

## Step 3: Name themes
A cluster becomes a theme when it has ≥3 instances across ≥2 users. Name with the user's frame:
- ✅ "Users expect save controls at the top of long forms"
- ❌ "Form UX"

## Step 4: Severity rate
For each theme:
- **Severity**: critical / serious / minor (does it block the task?)
- **Frequency**: % of users
- **Impact**: who is affected, what's the business cost

## Step 5: Recommendations
Each theme → 1–2 specific recommendations. Recommendations are actionable, not aspirational:
- ✅ "Move primary save button to top-right of long forms (>5 fields)"
- ❌ "Improve form usability"

## Validate
Send the cluster board + themes to one teammate who didn't run the study; do they reach similar themes? If not, you may be pattern-matching to confirmation.
