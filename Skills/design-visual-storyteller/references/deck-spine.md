# Deck spine templates

## Problem → Solution → Proof → Ask (B2B pitch, default)
1. Hook — one striking fact about the problem
2. Problem — who has it, why it matters, what they do today
3. Insight — your unique frame on the problem
4. Solution — what you built, in one diagram
5. Proof — case study / metric / quote
6. Ask — what you need next (meeting, pilot, decision)

## Before → After → Bridge (change announcement)
1. Before — current state with a clear pain
2. After — desired state with the metric that proves it
3. Bridge — the plan, in 3–5 steps
4. Ask

## Narrative arc (longer-form, exec update)
1. Setting — context the audience needs
2. Conflict — what's not working / what shifted
3. Quest — what we're trying to do
4. Surprise — what we learned that was non-obvious
5. Resolution — decisions made / asks open

## Customer story (case study)
1. Customer — who they are, in one line
2. Problem they had
3. What they tried (and why it didn't work)
4. What changed when they used you
5. Result, quantified
6. Quote
