# Copy voice — phrasing patterns that earn warmth

## Acknowledge the moment
Replace generic with specific. The user just did a thing — name it.

❌ "Welcome!" → ✅ "Welcome — first project, huh? Let's make it count."
❌ "Saved" → ✅ "Saved — 3:47pm."
❌ "Done!" → ✅ "Done. That was the last one in your inbox."

## Specificity over enthusiasm
Enthusiasm without specificity reads as forced. Specificity = real care.

❌ "🎉 Awesome! 🎉" → ✅ "Sent. 47 recipients."

## Concrete next step, not just praise
The whimsy beat is followed by an action, not a dead end.

❌ "Profile complete! 🎊" → ✅ "Profile complete. You're now searchable — try finding yourself."

## Warmth in errors
Frustration is the moment to be MOST careful. The user is already upset; piling on emoji makes it worse.

❌ "Oopsie! Something went wrong 😅" → ✅ "Couldn't reach the server. Your changes are saved locally — we'll retry automatically in a moment."

## Dry over cute, where the brand allows
A dry, specific observation beats forced cuteness, especially for professional tools.

❌ "Oh no! 😭" → ✅ "That's the third 500 from their API today."

## What to avoid in copy
- Exclamation marks > 1 per screen
- Multiple emoji per line
- "Awesome / amazing / fantastic" without specifics
- Pop culture refs ("It's like Schrödinger's button…")
- Forced humor that doesn't acknowledge the moment

## Brand voice consistency
Write 5–10 example whimsy moments together as a voice doc. Other writers refer to it. Variation across writers is the #1 reason whimsy reads as inconsistent.
