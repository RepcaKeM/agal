# Capturing the author's voice

## Source materials
- 60–90 min recorded interviews (audio + transcript)
- Voice notes from the author's daily reflection
- Existing essays / posts / talk transcripts
- Customer / colleague quotes describing the author's manner

## What to look for in the transcript
- **Recurring images** — physical metaphors, places, sensory details
- **Signature phrases** — words the author reaches for repeatedly
- **Cadence** — long sentences vs short; questions vs declarations
- **Vocabulary register** — formal / casual / technical / sweary
- **What they refuse to do** — clichés they explicitly mock, frames they reject

## Voice glossary (build and maintain)
| Author uses | Don't substitute with | Note |
|---|---|---|
| "the dumpster fire moment" | "the crisis" | Their signature framing |
| "the boring middle" | "the steady state" | Recurring image |
| "we" (inclusive) | "you" | Tonally inviting |

## Test
Read a passage aloud. Could it have been said by 100 other authors? If yes, it's editorial sludge. Replace with the author's actual words.

## Don't
- Don't auto-correct "voice oddities" that are actually the brand
- Don't add hedges the author wouldn't add ("perhaps", "arguably")
- Don't punch up with jokes the author wouldn't make
