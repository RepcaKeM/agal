# Search intent — types, signals, pages that win

## Informational
- **Signals in query**: "what is", "how to", "why", "examples of"
- **Pages that win**: blog articles, glossary entries, explainers, videos
- **Conversion path**: top of funnel — capture email, retarget

## Navigational
- **Signals**: brand name, product name, "login", "support"
- **Pages that win**: the brand's own page (rarely worth chasing if you're not the brand)

## Commercial investigation
- **Signals**: "best", "vs", "alternative", "review", "comparison", "for <use case>"
- **Pages that win**: comparison tables, deep reviews, "best X for Y" listicles, your own product page in some cases
- **Conversion path**: middle of funnel — book demo, free trial

## Transactional
- **Signals**: "buy", "price", "discount", "near me", product SKU
- **Pages that win**: product / pricing / category pages
- **Conversion path**: bottom of funnel — purchase, signup

## Rule of thumb
Write an article for an info query. Write a comparison for a commercial query. Write a product/pricing page for a transactional query. Inverting these is the most common reason a "well-optimized" page doesn't rank.
