# Nudge templates — by blocker (no dark patterns)

## Capability — don't know how

**Don't**: "Complete your profile!"
**Do**: Show a 1-minute walkthrough at the moment of need. Pre-fill what you can. Provide a worked example: "Here's what a complete profile looks like →".

Copy: instruction-first.
> "Add your portfolio URL to help collaborators find your work. Example: alex.example.com"

## Opportunity (physical) — wrong moment

**Don't**: Send a "complete this!" notification at 8am.
**Do**: Trigger at the moment they're already in context. ("You just published your first post — add a portfolio URL so readers can find more of your work?")

Copy: contextual.
> "While you're here — add a portfolio URL?"

## Opportunity (social) — no norm

**Don't**: "Complete your profile."
**Do**: Show peer behavior honestly (don't invent the percentage).
> "12 of your 23 teammates have linked their portfolios."

Caution: tipping point. Showing low numbers ("3 of 20") can DEMOTIVATE. Only use when the number is high enough to suggest norm.

## Motivation (reflective) — don't see value

**Don't**: Tell them it's important.
**Do**: Show a specific outcome that depends on the behavior.
> "Designers with completed portfolios get 4× more contact requests."

Cite real data; if you can't, find a different angle.

## Motivation (automatic) — habit not formed

**Don't**: Streak-shame.
**Do**: Acknowledge progress as it happens, reduce friction on the next step.
> "Nice — first post published. Two more taps to make it discoverable: add tags."

## Universal copy rules

- Acknowledge what they just did
- Name the next concrete action (1 verb + 1 object)
- Make the outcome specific and (where possible) social/observable
- Provide a real out ("Not now" with no penalty)
- Cap escalation: never "this is your 3rd reminder"
