# Competitive landscape — shapes that hold up

## 1. Capabilities matrix (best for "where do we stand?")

| Capability | Us | Competitor A | Competitor B | Competitor C |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cap 1 | ✅ best-in-class | ✅ | ⚠️ partial | ❌ |
| Cap 2 | ⚠️ | ✅ | ✅ | ⚠️ |
| Cap 3 | ❌ | ❌ | ⚠️ | ✅ |

Score honestly. Cite the source for each non-us cell. Don't mark "best-in-class" without evidence.

## 2. Positioning 2×2 (best for "where's the gap?")

Pick two axes that matter to customers (e.g. "depth of feature" vs "ease of setup"). Plot competitors. Look for empty quadrants — they're either an opportunity or a graveyard.
