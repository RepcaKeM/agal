# Source-quality stack (top is best)

## Tier 1 — Primary
- Customer interviews (your own + competitors' churned customers)
- First-party usage data
- Your sales-team call recordings (gold for "what customers actually ask for")

## Tier 2 — Analyst-grade
- Gartner / Forrester / IDC reports (read in full, not press summary)
- Public-company filings (10-K, S-1, earnings calls — search-engine for the disclosure)
- Academic papers (especially for emerging tech)

## Tier 3 — Reputable press
- Trade press with editorial standards (The Information, Stratechery, Pragmatic Engineer)
- Major business press (FT, WSJ, NYT)

## Tier 4 — Use with caution
- Vendor blogs and whitepapers (they're sales material)
- Conference talks (selection bias; vendors pay to speak)
- LinkedIn / Twitter posts (signal exists but noisy)

## Tier 5 — Anecdotes
- One-off blog posts, AI-summarized "industry trends"

## Rule
Triangulate: one Tier-2 source + one Tier-3 source is stronger than four Tier-4 sources saying the same thing.
