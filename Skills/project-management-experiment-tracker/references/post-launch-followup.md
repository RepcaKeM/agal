# Post-launch follow-up

## Week 1 after 100% rollout
- Sanity-check the primary metric matches the test's measured lift (small differences expected; large gaps are red flags).
- Re-check guardrails on the broader population.
- Compare new users (no exposure history) vs returning users.

## Week 4
- Did the lift hold? Novelty effects typically fade by week 2–3.
- If lift dropped > 30%: write a brief note, decide whether to keep, modify, or revert.
- Update the team's "lifts that held vs lifts that evaporated" log; calibrate future estimates from real history.

## Week 12
- Long-run impact on retention / LTV (if applicable).
- Surface any unintended interactions with other features launched since.

## Lesson capture
For every closed experiment (win or loss), write 3 lines:
- What we thought would happen
- What actually happened
- What we'd do differently

These accumulate into the team's intuition. Track them in a single doc; calibrate quarterly.
