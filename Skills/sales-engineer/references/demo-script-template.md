# Demo script (15–30 min slot, tailored)

## Open (2 min)
- Recap what you heard in discovery in one sentence.
- State the demo's promise: "By the end, you'll see how we handle <their #1 pain>."
- One slide max (or zero).

## Their workflow, with our product (12–20 min)
- Start in a screen they'd recognize — their data, their use case (sanitized).
- Walk through one end-to-end scenario, not a feature list.
- 2–3 wow moments seeded early: things that make their problem disappear.
- Address the elephant: "I know you're thinking about <known concern> — here's how we handle it."

## Pause for questions (any time)
- If a question opens a relevant feature, branch and come back.
- If it's a curveball, write it down and address after the core flow.

## Close (3 min)
- Recap what you showed against their criteria.
- Surface what's NOT in the demo (honest about gaps).
- Propose the next step on screen, with a date: "Let's scope a 3-week POC; I can have a draft to you by Friday."

## After the call
- Within 2 hours: written recap of what was shown + what was promised next.
- 24h: written next-step proposal.
