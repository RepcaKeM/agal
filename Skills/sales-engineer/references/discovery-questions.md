# Discovery questions — by phase

## Situation (where they are)
- "Walk me through how your team handles <task> today."
- "What tools are in that workflow?"
- "Who's involved, and what's each person's role?"
- "How long has the current setup been in place?"

## Pain (what's broken)
- "What's the part of that workflow that frustrates the team?"
- "When did you first notice this becoming a problem?"
- "What have you tried so far? What worked, what didn't?"
- "If we could only fix ONE thing, what would matter most?"

## Impact (what it costs)
- "What does that frustration cost — time, revenue, customer satisfaction?"
- "If this doesn't get fixed in the next 6 months, what happens?"
- "Who else feels this pain? How do they describe it?"

## Vision (what good looks like)
- "If we had a magic wand, what would the ideal workflow look like?"
- "How would you measure success 90 days after switching?"
- "What does your boss / CFO care about that this would move?"

## Process (how they buy)
- "Walk me through how decisions like this get made at your company."
- "Who else needs to bless this — security, legal, finance?"
- "What's the budget process? Is there money allocated, or do we need to build the case?"
- "What's your ideal timeline to get this in production?"

## Rules
- Open questions, not yes/no. "What…", "How…", "Walk me through…"
- Listen 70% of the time. Their words are the proposal you'll write.
- Take notes; play back what you heard at the end of the call.
