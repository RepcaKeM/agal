# 1-page proposal

**To**: <name, title>
**From**: <your name>
**Date**:

## The problem (in your words)
2–3 sentences. Their description of what's broken, played back to them. They should nod at this line.

## What we'll do
3–6 bullets, plain language. Not a feature list — outcomes.

## How we'll measure success
One primary metric, baseline now, target, deadline. (Optional: 1–2 guardrails.)

## What you'll do
Bi-directional. Their access, data, time, decision-making commitments. Without these, the project stalls.
