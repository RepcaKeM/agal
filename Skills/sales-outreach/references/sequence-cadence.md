# Sequence cadence — 7-touch over 18 days

| # | Day | Channel | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Email | Trigger-based intro | ≤120 words; low-friction ask |
| 2 | 2 | LinkedIn | Connection request, no pitch | "Saw your post on X — would love to stay in touch." |
| 3 | 5 | Email | Different angle on same pain | Reference a customer story or piece of content; not a re-hash |
| 4 | 8 | Phone | Live call (if appropriate) | If they don't pick up, leave a 15-sec voicemail; mention you'll email |
| 5 | 11 | Email | Send a useful artifact (no ask) | Article, teardown, or short loom relevant to their context |
| 6 | 14 | LinkedIn | Comment thoughtfully on their post | Show you're a human paying attention |
| 7 | 18 | Email | Breakup email | "Going to close the loop unless I hear back. Reply with 'no' or 'wrong time' and I'll respect it." |

## Rules
- If they reply at any point, the sequence stops. Resume only by hand, never by automation.
- If the breakup email gets no response, the account moves back to nurture (quarterly), not deletion.
- After 2 full sequences with no reply across 6 months → re-qualify the account before any further outreach.

## Channel etiquette
- Don't auto-DM on LinkedIn. Send connection request with a one-line context, then wait.
- Don't leave voicemail at executive level (they don't check); call mobile only if a customer gave you the number.
- Don't send images / PDFs in cold email — go to spam, and they don't render on phones.
