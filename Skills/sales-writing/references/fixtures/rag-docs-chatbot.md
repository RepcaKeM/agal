# Internal chatbot over our contracts and procedures (RAG)

**Budget:** Fixed price, $2,500 · **Experience:** Intermediate

We are an accounting firm with around 40 staff. Our procedures, client contracts and
internal guidelines are spread across about 3,000 PDFs and Word files on SharePoint,
some of them scanned. New employees constantly ask senior staff where to find things
and what a given contract says about deadlines or fees.

We want an internal chatbot that answers questions using only our documents and shows
the exact document and page each answer comes from. Data must stay in the EU, ideally
on our own Azure tenant or server. Scanned PDFs need OCR. We would like a first working
version on a subset of documents within four weeks.

**Screening questions**
1. Which stack would you use for this and why?
2. How will users know whether an answer can be trusted?
